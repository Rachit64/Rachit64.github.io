About Me

I'm Rachit, a third-year Information Technology student at MIT Manipal. I like taking an idea all the way from a model or a schema to something people can actually click on — which is how I ended up building a serverless AWS storefront and a multi-agent AI platform in the same year.

Right now I'm an AI intern at Trueld, teaching deep-learning and OCR models to read messy Arabic banking documents. I care about systems that stay honest when the data is bad: server-side checks, layered fallbacks, and agents that only reason over cited signals.

Away from the keyboard you'll find me in the pool, at the gym or on the basketball court, or helping run competitive events at Revels, our cultural fest.
